About

Real planets, real orbits, no made-up moons.

Known Orbits is a one-person shop in Philadelphia that turns real star systems into shirts, prints and desktop wallpapers. The drawings come from code, written from the measurements.

The orbits come from the NASA Exoplanet Archive, and the planet positions are checked against NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL). The positions for January 1, 2027 are calculated from JPL's published orbital elements. The background stars come from a U.S. Naval Observatory star catalog.

Anything that isn't confirmed gets labeled. Proxima c is dashed and says unconfirmed. If the distances had to be squished so Neptune fits on a shirt, the shirt says so. Nobody has seen the surface of a planet around another star, so these planets are plain globes.

Custom programs draw every one of them, frame by frame for the wallpapers. Every source is on the credits page.

One shirt uses public-domain art: Ernst Haeckel's 1904 jellyfish plate, cleaned up so it prints well.

A print partner makes each shirt to order and ships it in the US. The wallpapers are 4K loops on itch.io and download right away.

Shirts are paused for now. Prints and wallpapers are available today.

Spot a mistake in the data, or want a system that isn't here yet? Send a message to hello@knownorbits.com.
