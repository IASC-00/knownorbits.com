Shipping and returns

Last updated November 20, 2026.

How shirts ship, and what happens if something's wrong.

Shirts

Each shirt is made to order by our print partner and ships to US addresses only. Each Etsy listing states how long a shirt usually takes to ship.

Because every shirt is made just for you, returns follow the Etsy listing policy: we offer exchanges or refunds only for defects or wrong items. Ask within 30 days of delivery and send a photo.

Digital files

Digital files can't be returned. If a file is faulty or doesn't match its description, message the shop and we'll fix it or refund you.

Buyers in the EU and UK: by completing your purchase, you ask for the download to start right away and you acknowledge that you lose your 14-day right of withdrawal once it starts.

Help with an order

For an order, message the shop through Etsy or itch.io, which is the fastest way to get help. Otherwise write to hello@knownorbits.com. See the contact page.
