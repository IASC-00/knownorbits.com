Printing, shipping and wallpapers

Shirts and prints

How are the shirts made?

Each one is printed to order by our print partner after you buy it, so nothing sits in a warehouse.

How long does it take?

Most orders ship within 2 to 7 business days. Delivery after that depends on the carrier. You'll get tracking as soon as it ships.

Where do you ship?

Shipping is free in the US. We don't ship outside the US yet.

Which size should I get?

The shirts are a unisex cut. Each Etsy listing has a size chart with measurements. Please check it before you order, since every shirt is printed just for you.

What are they made of, and how do I wash them?

Solid colors are 100% combed and ring-spun cotton. Heather colors are cotton and polyester blends. The listing has the details. Wash cold, inside out, and tumble dry low to keep the print fresh.

Can I return a shirt?

Because each shirt is printed for you, we can't take returns or exchanges for size or a change of mind. If your shirt arrives damaged, misprinted, or isn't what you ordered, message us within 30 days of delivery with a photo and we'll send a replacement or a full refund. You don't need to send it back.

Live wallpapers

What's in the download?

The wallpaper as an MP4 video in 4K (3840×2160), 1440p and 1080p, a 60-second loop with no sound, plus three still images at 4K and a README with setup steps, the credits and the license. Pick the file that matches your screen, or the next size up.

How do I set one as my wallpaper?

Windows: in Wallpaper Engine, choose Open Wallpaper, then Open from file, and pick the MP4. In Lively Wallpaper (free in the Microsoft Store), click + and choose the MP4. With no app, right-click one of the still images and choose Set as desktop background.

Mac: macOS doesn't play your own videos as a wallpaper by itself. Use a video wallpaper app from the Mac App Store and point it at the MP4, or set a still image in System Settings, Wallpaper.

Linux: on GNOME, the free Hidamari app plays a video as your wallpaper. On KDE Plasma, add a video wallpaper plugin. Or set a still image in your desktop's background settings.

Turn on looping if your player asks. The files are made to loop. Most wallpaper apps can pause the video when a window covers the desktop, which saves battery.

What can I do with the files?

Use them as a wallpaper or screensaver on any devices you own or use yourself, at home or at work, and keep backup copies. Please don't sell, share or re-upload them, or use them in something you publish or sell, without asking first. Images from other people inside the scenes keep their own licenses; see the credits. The full terms are in LICENSE.txt in the download.

Does itch.io let me pay more?

Yes. The itch.io price is the minimum, and you can pay more.
