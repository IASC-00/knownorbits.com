Thanksgiving 2026 night sky: what to see after dinner

After dinner on Thanksgiving, Thursday, November 26, 2026, look east-northeast for the Moon. It rises around 6 PM and is still 92% lit, two days after full. Turn south and the bright, steady point about halfway up is Saturn, and if you stay up late, Jupiter rises around 10:20 PM with Mars close behind.

We set the sky for 7 PM over Plymouth, Massachusetts. From most of the US it looks much the same after dinner. The Moon just comes up a little earlier or later depending on where you are.

The Moon, two days after full

The Moon was full at 9:53 AM Eastern on Tuesday, November 24. By Thanksgiving evening it's a waning gibbous, rising at 6:06 PM in Plymouth and sitting low in the east-northeast by 7. It loses about 6% of its lit face a day from here. Capella, the Pleiades and Aldebaran sit above it, so it makes a handy pointer.

Saturn, the steady one

Saturn is about 47° up in the south-southeast at 7 PM, at magnitude +0.6. It sets around 2 AM.

Look west for Vega, Deneb and Altair. Low in the south sits Fomalhaut.

If you're up late

Jupiter clears the east-northeast horizon around 10:20 PM in Plymouth, and Mars follows around 10:40 PM. Venus is a morning planet this week, up around 3:26 AM.
