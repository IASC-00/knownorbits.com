Night sky 2026: what's up and when

The Geminid meteor shower peaks on Sunday night, December 13, into Monday morning, and the winter solstice is Monday, December 21, at 20:50 UT. These guides give the real sky for those nights and the holidays around them, with times you can step outside and use.

Every star and planet here is placed from a star catalog or NASA JPL's ephemeris, and cross-checked against the US Naval Observatory or NASA JPL Horizons wherever those could be looked up. Anything that isn't data, like a cartoon sleigh or an illustrative meteor streak, is labeled.

The guides

  • Thanksgiving 2026: what to see after dinnerThursday, November 26. The Moon rises around 6 PM, 92% lit, and Saturn sits high in the south.
  • Geminids 2026: when and where to watchSunday night, December 13, into Monday. Look east after 9 PM; the radiant is almost overhead by 2 AM.
  • Winter solstice 2026 and Stonehenge's skyMonday, December 21. The midwinter sunset has moved 1° since 2500 BC, and the pole star was Thuban.
  • Christmas Eve 2026: what's up at 9 PMThursday, December 24. The Moon in the east, Orion and Sirius in the southeast, Saturn in the southwest.
