Real star systems, drawn to their measured orbits

These are real planetary systems, drawn from published measurements: the planets move at their measured periods, and anything unconfirmed is labeled unconfirmed. Each page has the plain facts and a video, and most have a live wallpaper.

Nobody has seen the surface of a planet around another star, so the planets are plain globes.

The systems

  • TRAPPIST-1Seven Earth-size planets around a small red star, 40 light-years away. A year there lasts 1.5 to 19 days.
  • Kepler-16A planet with two suns. The stars circle each other every 41 days; the planet circles both every 229.
  • HD 110067Six planets whose years keep a nearly exact rhythm: 3:2, 3:2, 3:2, 4:3, 4:3.
  • Alpha CentauriThe nearest star system, 4.3 light-years away. A and B next pass closest in 2035.
  • The Solar SystemOur own planets at their true relative speeds, from Mercury's 88 days to Neptune's 165 years.
  • Sagittarius A*Stars on measured orbits around the black hole at the center of our galaxy, 27,000 light-years away.
