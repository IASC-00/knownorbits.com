Privacy

Last updated November 21, 2026.

This site sets no cookies and does no tracking. It counts visits anonymously, and that's all.

What this site does

It's a set of plain pages. One script works out the Moon's phase inside your browser and sends nothing anywhere. The other counts the visit (see Analytics below). The fonts and images are served from this site, not from anyone else's.

Like any web host, the server that delivers these pages may keep short-lived technical logs (such as IP addresses) to keep the site running and safe. We don't use them to identify or follow anyone.

Analytics

We count visits with GoatCounter, a cookie-free counter that doesn't track individuals. When a page loads, it records the page, the site that sent you (if any), your browser, screen size and language, and your rough country. It doesn't store your IP address, set cookies or follow you to other sites. We use the totals only to see which pages people read.

Newsletter

When the newsletter opens, signing up takes your email address and nothing else. We use it only to send Known Orbits updates: new systems, prints and wallpapers. Every email has an unsubscribe link. We never sell or share the list.

To have your address deleted, unsubscribe or email hello@knownorbits.com and we'll remove it.

Buying

You don't buy anything on this site. Shirts and prints are sold on Etsy and live wallpapers on itch.io. When you order, your name, address and payment are handled by them under their own privacy policies: Etsy's privacy policy and itch.io's privacy policy. We see what each shop shares with sellers to fill your order, such as your shipping address for a shirt, and we use it only for that.

Email to us

If you write to hello@knownorbits.com, we use your message only to reply.

Do Not Track

The visit counter doesn't identify you or follow you, so a "do not track" signal doesn't change what it records. No third party tracks you across sites through this site.

Children

This site isn't aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top changes with it.
